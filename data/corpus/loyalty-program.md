# Brightleaf Rewards

## Earning points
Members earn **2 points per $1** spent on Brightleaf-branded products and **1 point per $1** on all other products. Points post 30 days after delivery, once the return window has closed.

## Tiers
- **Member**: free, from the first purchase.
- **Plus**: after $1,000 spent in a calendar year. Plus members get free expedited shipping.
- **Pro Installer**: by application for licensed integrators. Pro Installer members get net-30 invoicing and dealer pricing.

## Redeeming
Every **500 points** can be redeemed for a $10 reward. Rewards expire **12 months** after they are issued.

## Returns and points
Points earned on a returned item are deducted. If the balance is not sufficient, the value is deducted from the refund.
