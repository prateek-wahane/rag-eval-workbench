# Contacting Support

## Hours
Phone and chat support are available **Monday to Friday, 6 AM to 6 PM Pacific**, and **Saturday 8 AM to 2 PM Pacific**. Support is closed on Sundays and US federal holidays.

## Channels
- Chat: on every product page and in the Brightleaf Connect app
- Phone: 1-800-555-0142
- Email: help@brightleaf.example (replies within 1 business day)

## Pro Installer support
Pro Installers have a dedicated priority line with extended hours until **8 PM Pacific** on weekdays.

## Technical support for third-party products
For third-party products we provide setup guidance only; repairs and warranty claims go to the manufacturer.
