# Brightleaf In-Ceiling Speaker C6

## Overview
The C6 is a 6.5-inch two-way in-ceiling speaker sold in pairs.

## Specs
- Frequency response: **45 Hz to 20 kHz**
- Impedance: **8 ohms**
- Recommended amplifier power: 10 to 100 W per channel
- Cut-out diameter: **8.2 inches**
- Paintable magnetic grille

## Installation
Requires 16-gauge speaker wire for runs under 50 feet and 14-gauge for longer runs. A pre-construction bracket is sold separately.

## Warranty
The C6 is a standard Brightleaf product.
