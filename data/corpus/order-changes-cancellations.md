# Changing or Cancelling an Order

## Cancellation window
You can cancel an order online for **60 minutes** after it is placed, as long as its status is still "Processing". After that, contact support; if the order has already been picked at the warehouse, it must be returned instead.

## Changing the shipping address
Address changes are only possible before the order ships. Once a tracking number is issued the address cannot be changed by Brightleaf, but you may request a redirect from the carrier.

## Backordered items
If an item is backordered, the order page shows an estimated ship date (ETA). Orders with backordered items are held and ship complete unless you choose **Ship Available Items Now**, which splits the order at no extra shipping cost.

## Partial cancellations
You may cancel individual backordered lines at any time before they ship without affecting the rest of the order.
