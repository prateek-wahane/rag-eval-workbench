# Price Match Guarantee

## Who is eligible
Brightleaf matches the price of identical, in-stock new items sold by authorized US retailers.

## Window
Price match requests are accepted before purchase and up to **14 days after delivery**.

## Exclusions
Marketplace sellers, auction sites, B-Stock and open-box items, bundle offers, and coupon-code prices are excluded. Price matches are limited to **3 units per item per customer**.

## How to request
Use the chat on the product page or email pricematch@brightleaf.example with a link to the competitor listing. Approved matches are refunded as the price difference to the original payment method.
