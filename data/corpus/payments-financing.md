# Payment Methods and Financing

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal and Brightleaf gift cards. Pro Installer accounts may also pay by net-30 invoice.

## When you are charged
Your card is authorized when you place the order and **charged when the order ships**. Split shipments are charged separately as each package ships.

## Financing
Orders over **$499** qualify for 0% APR financing for **12 months** through our financing partner, subject to credit approval. Payments are made to the financing partner, not Brightleaf.

## Sales tax
Sales tax is charged based on the shipping address. Tax-exempt customers must upload a certificate before checkout; tax is not refunded retroactively.
