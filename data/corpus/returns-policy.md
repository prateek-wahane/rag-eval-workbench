# Returns Policy

## Standard return window
Brightleaf Home accepts returns of most items within **30 days** of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging with all accessories, manuals and mounting hardware.

## Extended holiday returns
Orders delivered between **November 15 and December 24** may be returned until **January 31** of the following year.

## Non-returnable items
The following cannot be returned: custom-cut cables, opened software licenses, gift cards, clearance items marked "Final Sale", and smart locks once they have been paired to an account.

## Restocking fee
Opened networking equipment and opened smart-home hubs carry a **15% restocking fee**. Unopened items have no restocking fee. Items returned because they arrived defective are never charged a restocking fee.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5 business days** after the item is received and inspected at our returns center in Reno, Nevada. Store credit is issued instantly upon inspection.

## How to start a return
Log in, open **Orders**, choose the item and select **Start a Return**. A prepaid label is emailed for orders over $99; for orders under $99 a flat **$7.95** return-shipping fee is deducted from the refund.
