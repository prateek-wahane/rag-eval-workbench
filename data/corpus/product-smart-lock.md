# Brightleaf Smart Lock SL2

## Overview
The SL2 is a keypad and fingerprint deadbolt that works with Wi-Fi and Matter.

## Batteries
The SL2 uses **4 AA** batteries and lasts about **8 months** with typical use. A low-battery warning appears in the app when about 2 weeks of power remain. A 9V battery can be held against the emergency terminals under the keypad to power it temporarily.

## Access codes
The SL2 stores up to **100 access codes** and **50 fingerprints**. Temporary codes can be scheduled for specific days and hours.

## Auto-lock
Auto-lock can be set between 30 seconds and 10 minutes after the door closes.

## Returns
Because it stores security credentials, a smart lock cannot be returned once it has been paired to an account. Defective paired locks are handled through the warranty process.
