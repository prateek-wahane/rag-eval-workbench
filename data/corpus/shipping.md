# Shipping Options and Costs

## Warehouses
Orders ship from three warehouses: **Reno, Nevada**; **Columbus, Ohio**; and **Charlotte, North Carolina**. The system picks the warehouse closest to you that has all items in stock.

## Shipping speeds
| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3-5 business days | Free on orders over $49, otherwise $6.99 |
| Expedited | 2 business days | $14.99 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $29.99 |

## Order cut-off
Orders placed before **2 PM Pacific time** on a business day ship the same day. Orders after the cut-off ship the next business day.

## Freight items
Items over 70 lb (such as large equipment racks) ship by freight with curbside delivery. Freight orders are scheduled by phone and take 5-8 business days.

## International shipping
Brightleaf Home ships only to the United States and Canada. Canadian orders are charged duties and taxes at checkout and take 5-9 business days.
