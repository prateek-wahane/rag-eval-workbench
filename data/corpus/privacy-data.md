# Privacy and Your Data

## What we collect
We collect your name, contact details, order history, and, for smart-home devices, device status and event logs.

## Smart device data retention
Smart lock event logs (lock/unlock history) are kept for **90 days** and then deleted. Router traffic statistics are kept for **30 days**. Video is never stored by Brightleaf.

## Deleting your account
You can request account deletion under **Account > Privacy**. Deletion is completed within **30 days**. Order records are kept for **7 years** for tax purposes even after account deletion.

## Marketing emails
You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any marketing email. Order and security emails cannot be turned off.
