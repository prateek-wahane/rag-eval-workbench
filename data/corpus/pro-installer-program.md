# Pro Installer Program

## Eligibility
The Pro Installer program is for licensed low-voltage contractors and AV integrators with a valid business license and tax ID.

## Benefits
- Dealer pricing, typically **25% below** retail on Brightleaf products.
- **Net-30 invoicing** with a starting credit limit of $5,000, reviewed every 6 months.
- A dedicated account manager and priority phone support.
- Free **demo units** (up to 2 per product line per year) for showrooms.

## Application
Applications are reviewed within **5 business days**. Required documents: business license, tax exemption certificate (if applicable) and one trade reference.

## Returns for Pro Installers
Pro Installers have a **45-day** return window on unopened products. The 15% restocking fee on opened networking equipment still applies.
