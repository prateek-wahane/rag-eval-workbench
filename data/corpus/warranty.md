# Warranty Coverage

## Brightleaf branded products
All Brightleaf-branded products (speakers, mounts, cables and network switches) include a **2-year limited warranty** from the date of delivery. Brightleaf Pro series products include a **5-year limited warranty**.

## Third-party brands
Products made by other manufacturers carry the manufacturer's warranty only. Brightleaf will help you contact the manufacturer but does not process third-party warranty claims.

## What is not covered
Damage from improper installation, power surges without a surge protector, liquid exposure, and cosmetic wear are not covered.

## Filing a claim
Email warranty@brightleaf.example with your order number, serial number and a short video showing the issue. Approved claims receive an advance replacement shipped within **3 business days**; you return the faulty unit within 30 days using the included label.

## Warranty vs. return
Within the first 30 days a defective item should be returned under the Returns Policy for a full refund. After 30 days, use the warranty process.
