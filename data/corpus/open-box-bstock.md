# Open-Box and B-Stock Items

## What B-Stock means
B-Stock items are customer returns or demo units that passed a **27-point inspection**. They may have minor cosmetic marks but are fully functional.

## Pricing
B-Stock items are typically discounted **20% to 40%** off the new price. The discount is shown on the product page under the "B-Stock" tab.

## Warranty on B-Stock
B-Stock Brightleaf products carry a **1-year limited warranty**, half the standard Brightleaf warranty. Third-party B-Stock items carry a 90-day Brightleaf warranty.

## Returns on B-Stock
B-Stock items can be returned within **15 days** of delivery. The restocking fee does not apply to B-Stock returns.

## Availability
B-Stock inventory is limited and stocked only at the Reno warehouse. It is not eligible for same-day or in-store pickup.
